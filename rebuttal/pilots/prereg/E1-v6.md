# E1 pre-registration v6: N = 80 locked, LLM-judge labelling with a 30-instance author anchor (planning default)

2026-11-08. Supersedes v5 (`E1-v5.md`, unchanged). The N = 80 held-out draw, strata, weights and ID list are exactly those of v5 (locked manifest SHA-256 `e0191ee815bde0af4561c75b41f239a3bb3e91642bac3d0d397bf2eb66c609b1`). What changes: the 80 instances are labelled by an LLM judge, and authors label only a 30-instance anchor. This is a planning default until the authors confirm the details. No judge labelling has been run; no human label exists.

## Judge and anchor

- **Judge:** `google/gemini-3.6-flash` labels all 80 held-out instances × 3 base models (240 traces), one call per trace, with the frozen prompt `E1_fact_labelling` in `rebuttal/judge/judge_prompts_v1.json` (file SHA-256 `8b257aaf78cac6cd38380e2e543efb8677ac708289b72fe6ec13ebe75c61fa07`; prompts hash `e879635829dc8ddff198d31e4aea5b9f415852fb28e63e1ed93c0e74b14a649c`). The judge never sees matcher, lexical or audit output. Judge labels are always called judge labels and are never presented as human labels.
- **Anchor:** 30 of the 80 instances (all three models), each labelled by two authors (default pair rotation A+B / C+D alternating along the SHA order), independently and blind to judge and matcher output, then adjudicated. Draw rule (fixed before any label): allocate 30 across the six locked strata in proportion to the locked n_h (largest remainder; ties broken by ascending stratum name); within each stratum take the first n_anchor IDs of the locked SHA order. Anchor weight w_i = N_h / n_anchor (inverse overall inclusion probability n_anchor / N_h), used for every anchor-only estimate. Weights for the all-80 analysis stay as in v5.

## What is reported

- **On the 30-instance anchor** (weighted, 95% instance-cluster bootstrap CIs, 2,000 draws, seed 42): human–human Cohen κ; judge–human κ and accuracy; matcher–human accuracy; all for fact presence (primary) and fidelity. Fidelity gets Gwet AC1 beside κ, as in v5, with the same fixed categories (absent, faithful, partial, distorted).
- **On all 80 instances:** matcher–judge agreement (presence accuracy and κ; fidelity accuracy and AC1), weighted as in v5.
- Secondary: per-trace *lost* versus *reached but unused* call and the reweighted headline rates (including MuSiQue's 68%), now from judge labels with the anchor-based judge–human agreement reported beside them.
- No pass/fail thresholds. Fidelity κ's interval is wide because nearly every fact comes through intact, so a wide κ is not evidence of poor agreement.
- **Claim wording:** the matcher is "validated against author labels on a 30-instance anchor and compared with an LLM judge's labels on 80 instances". It is not described as validated on 80 human-labelled instances.
