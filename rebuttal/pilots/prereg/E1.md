## What is reported

- **On the 30-instance anchor** (weighted, 95% instance-cluster bootstrap CIs, 2,000 draws, seed 42): human–human Cohen κ; judge–human κ and accuracy; matcher–human accuracy; all for fact presence (primary) and fidelity. Fidelity gets Gwet AC1 beside κ, as in v5, with the same fixed categories (absent, faithful, partial, distorted).
- **On all 80 instances:** matcher–judge agreement (presence accuracy and κ; fidelity accuracy and AC1), weighted as in v5.
- Secondary: per-trace *lost* versus *reached but unused* call and the reweighted headline rates (including MuSiQue's 68%), now from judge labels with the anchor-based judge–human agreement reported beside them.
- No pass/fail thresholds. Fidelity κ's interval is wide because nearly every fact comes through intact, so a wide κ is not evidence of poor agreement.
- **Claim wording:** the matcher is "validated against author labels on a 30-instance anchor and compared with an LLM judge's labels on 80 instances". It is not described as validated on 80 human-labelled instances.

## Granularity-cap exclusions (new in v8, pre-registered)

For graph, ranking and other structured Silo-Bench runs the labelling guideline uses one joint fact per agent and a time cap of about 30 minutes per run; an author who cannot finish gives `exclude_uncertain` and writes "time cap" in `uncertain_reason`. **Reported with the E1 results:**
- the number of **excluded anchor instances per benchmark** (MuSiQue and Silo-Bench), as a count and a share of that benchmark's anchor instances;
- for each excluded instance: its **task type** and its **number of agents**.

**Flag.** If more than 10% of the Silo-Bench anchor is excluded, the report flags it as a **limitation**. **The Silo-Bench anchor has 8 instances, so one exclusion already exceeds 10%** (1 of 8 is 12.5%). Every percentage is shown **next to its actual count** (for example "1 of 8 instances, 12.5%"), never alone.

**The concern being flagged:** the cap removes the hardest cases from the κ computation, so κ on Silo-Bench may look better than it is. No analysis is added to compensate; the exclusions are counted, shown and flagged. Primary agreement figures still exclude partial and uncertain rows, as in v7.
