## What is measured

**Primary: fact presence** (is each critical fact on the board?). Human-human Cohen kappa and matcher accuracy against adjudicated human labels, each with a 95% instance-cluster-bootstrap CI. Simulated expected half-widths at N = 80: kappa about +-0.09 (0.077-0.105), accuracy about +-0.05 (0.04-0.054) (`20261108_review3/e1_interval_width.json`).

**Also reported: fidelity accuracy** (is a present fact faithful, partial or distorted?), expected half-width about +-0.075 (0.05-0.077).

**Secondary:**
- Fidelity kappa, with Gwet AC1 beside it on the same rows, weights and bootstrap (AC1 expected half-width 0.024-0.058). Fidelity kappa's interval is wide (about 0.12-0.25) because nearly every fact comes through intact, so a wide kappa is not evidence of poor agreement. AC1 is fixed before labels: weighted observed agreement P_o; pooled mean of the two weighted human category marginals p_c; P_e = sum p_c(1-p_c)/(q-1); AC1 = (P_o-P_e)/(1-P_e). Categories: absent, faithful, partial, distorted (q = 4). Uncertain rows are counted and excluded, never recoded. Formula source: Gwet's AgreeStat guide, section 4.4. AC1 is reported beside kappa, not as a replacement chosen after seeing results.
- Per-trace call: **lost** (a critical fact never reached the board) versus **reached but unused** (it reached the board but the answer did not use it), from human labels.
- Reweighted headline rates from human labels, including the MuSiQue candidate-aggregation-failure rate that the lexical rule puts at 68%, re-estimated with the instance weights below. These are reported with 95% CIs next to the lexical figure; they replace nothing silently.
- Partial-as-lost sensitivity: the primary excludes partial and uncertain rows (reported separately); the sensitivity counts partial as lost.

No pass/fail thresholds. Failed matcher labels stay errors. The 20 dev instances are used for matcher development only and are not in the sample.

## Sampling design

Frame: 85 MQ and 47 SB non-dev instances. Strata: benchmark x lexical coverage at strict tau = 0.5 (zero, partial, complete). Allocation: 60 MQ + 20 SB; equal round-robin within nonempty strata, capped at the stratum population. Order: ascending SHA256 of canonical JSON ["E1-lexical-v3", 42, stratum, instance_id], ID as tie-break; the first n_h are taken. Inclusion probability pi_h = n_h/N_h; weight w_i = N_h/n_h; every model and fact inherits its instance weight. Weighted kappa, accuracy and AC1 use 2,000 within-stratum instance-cluster bootstrap draws, seed 42, percentile 95% CIs, no finite-population correction; undefined-kappa draws are reported.
